# My review checklist

## Read first

What to read or run before judging anything.

## Check, in this order

1. **Correctness.** What would make it wrong for the people who use it.
2. **Safety.** What could leak, break or be lost.
3. **Clarity.** What will confuse the next reader.
4. **Style.** Only what the house style asks for.

## How to report

- One finding per item: where, what happens, how to fix it.
- Say what you checked and found fine, briefly.
- Leave out taste unless asked.
